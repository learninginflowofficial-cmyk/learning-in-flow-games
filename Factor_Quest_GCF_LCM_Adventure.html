📖 Two ideas to remember

GCF = Greatest Common Factor

Factors divide into a number evenly. Find the biggest factor both numbers share.

Example: 12 and 18
12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
Shared: 1, 2, 3, 6
GCF = 6

LCM = Least Common Multiple

Multiples come from skip counting. Find the first positive multiple both numbers share.

Example: 4 and 6
4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20…
6: 6, 12, 18, 24…
LCM = 12

Memory trick: GCF = biggest shared factor. LCM = smallest shared multiple.
