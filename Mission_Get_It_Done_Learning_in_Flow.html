Teacher notes

Goal: Practice task initiation, prioritization, time estimation, flexible thinking, and reflection. Suitable for individual or whole-class play.

Supports: No countdown, simple choices, readable task cards, and opportunities to revise. Students can use paper, visuals, or verbal responses.

Points: Awarded for completing each planning step, making an adaptive choice, and reflecting—not for speed or perfection.

Privacy: This game does not send or collect student data. Scores and answers are kept in this browser tab and reset when reloaded.
